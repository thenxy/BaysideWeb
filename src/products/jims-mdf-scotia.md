Every floating floor needs an expansion gap at the wall and MDF Scotia is the neatest way to cover it. This moisture-resistant MDF moulding sits along the wall-to-floor junction with a smooth, rounded profile that hides the gap, tidies up any imperfections at the edge of the installation, and gives the whole room a properly finished look.
