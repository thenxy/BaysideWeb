There's a reason wool has been the benchmark for quality carpet for as long as anyone can remember, nothing else quite matches it for feel, resilience, or the way it holds colour over time. Wool Weave is 100% wool, woven in a level loop pile on the diagonal with a subtle fleck running throughout that gives the surface genuine visual interest without competing with the room around it.
